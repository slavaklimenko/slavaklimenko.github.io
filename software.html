Software

Open, reproducible analysis

I use Python to create publicly available tools for the spectral analysis of absorption lines in quasar spectra, for modelling the physical conditions in interstellar clouds, and for analysing dust extinction and dust features in JWST spectra.

DE

Dust-extinction modelling

Fitting ultraviolet-to-infrared extinction curves along quasar sightlines and comparing carbonaceous, silicate, and ice absorption across galaxy environments.

  • UV–NIR extinction-curve fitting
  • 2175 Å bump parametrisation
  • Silicate and ice feature analysis (JWST/MIRI)
PythonRepository →
ISM

ISM physical conditions

Excitation modelling of H2 and CO rotational levels and C I fine-structure populations to infer temperature, density, and ultraviolet radiation intensity.

  • Level-population models for H2, C I, CO
  • Joint constraints from multiple tracers
  • CMB-temperature estimates from CO
PythonRepository →
SP

Spectroscopic workflows

Reproducible pipelines for absorption-line measurements, multiwavelength continuum analysis, model comparison, and publication-ready figures.

  • Absorption-line fitting
  • Continuum modelling across UV–IR
  • Figure generation for papers

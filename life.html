Life

Beyond the science

Outside of work, I spend time with my family and stay active. I enjoy rock climbing, skiing, orienteering, and running. This helps me relax, build resilience, and stay motivated during challenging times.

Family

The core of my life, keeping me grounded and motivated.

Rock climbing

I climb at the 8A/5.13 level and enjoy challenging routes. My favourite places to climb in the U.S. are Red River Gorge and Rumney.

Mountaineering

Mountains are a special passion of mine. I have climbed Elbrus and Mont Blanc, and I enjoy competing in trail and skyrunning races.

Running

I used to compete in races from 10 km to the marathon, with a personal best of under 2:30 in the marathon. I still enjoy running and keeping active.

Orienteering

Forest, map, compass, course — go. I enjoy the fast decisions, freedom, and challenge of unfamiliar terrain. Top 50 at the 10Mila relay (Sweden).

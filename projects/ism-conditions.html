Research project 02

Physical conditions in the cold interstellar medium

Joint modelling of H2, CO, and C I excitation with the Meudon PDR code.

Research overview

I developed a method to infer physical conditions in molecular gas by jointly fitting the rotational excitation of CO and H2, together with the fine-structure excitation of C I, using Meudon PDR models. The method self-consistently incorporates gas chemistry, thermal balance, radiative transfer, and photon trapping in optically thick CO lines. It yields constraints on the gas density, kinetic temperature, thermal pressure, and UV radiation field from absorption-line measurements.

Applied to the Galactic ISM and high-redshift absorbers, this method traces how physical conditions evolve with increasing molecular content. We directly measure changes in the gas density, from ∼100 to ∼300–1000 cm−3, and in kinetic temperature, from ∼100 to ∼30 K, across the transition from diffuse atomic to dense molecular gas.

Research project 02

The baryon cycle around galaxies

Connecting cold circumgalactic gas with the spatially resolved properties of the galaxies that host it.

Research overview

By combining HST/COS quasar absorption spectra with SDSS/MaNGA integral-field maps, I connect cold circumgalactic gas to galaxy rotation, metallicity gradients, star formation, and the flow of material into and out of galaxies.

A pilot sample showed co-rotation between the inner CGM and galaxy disks, and metallicities consistent with disk gradients. The expanded sample targets roughly 50 systems within the virial radius at z = 0.02–0.1.

Observational approach

Quasar absorption spectroscopy provides sensitive measurements of the gas surrounding galaxies, while integral-field spectroscopy provides spatially resolved information about the galaxies themselves.

Key connections

  • Circumgalactic gas and galaxy rotation
  • Gas-phase metallicity gradients
  • Spatially resolved star formation
  • Gas accretion onto galaxies
  • Galactic outflows

Research project 02

The baryon cycle around galaxies

I connect cold circumgalactic gas with the spatially resolved properties of the galaxies that host it.

Research overview

By combining HST/COS quasar absorption spectra with SDSS/MaNGA integral-field maps, I connect cold circumgalactic gas to galaxy rotation, metallicity gradients, star formation, and the flow of material into and out of galaxies.

Observational approach

Quasar absorption spectroscopy provides sensitive measurements of gas surrounding galaxies, while integral-field spectroscopy provides spatially resolved information about the galaxies themselves.

Key connections

  • Circumgalactic gas and galaxy rotation
  • Gas-phase metallicity gradients
  • Spatially resolved star formation
  • Gas accretion onto galaxies
  • Galactic outflows

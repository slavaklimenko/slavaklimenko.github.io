Research project 01

Molecular gas in the early Universe

I study H2, CO, and neutral carbon absorption in damped Lyα systems at z ≈ 2–3.

Research overview

Excitation modeling constrains gas temperature, density, ultraviolet radiation, chemical enrichment, and the cosmic microwave background temperature.

Observational approach

This research uses absorption spectroscopy of distant background quasars to detect and characterize cold gas that can be difficult to observe directly in emission.

Measurements

  • Molecular hydrogen absorption
  • Carbon monoxide absorption
  • Neutral carbon fine-structure excitation
  • Gas temperature and density
  • Ultraviolet radiation fields

Research project 03

Dust composition and extinction

I investigate the composition and optical properties of cosmic dust using ultraviolet-to-mid-infrared observations.

Research overview

I use JWST mid-infrared spectroscopy and ultraviolet-to-near-infrared data to measure extinction curves, the 2175 Å carbonaceous feature, and silicate and ice absorption in galaxies at z ≈ 0.1–1.3.

Observational approach

Combining observations across a broad wavelength range makes it possible to connect extinction-curve shapes with specific dust and molecular absorption features.

Measurements

  • Ultraviolet-to-near-infrared extinction curves
  • The 2175 Å carbonaceous feature
  • Silicate absorption
  • Ice absorption
  • Mid-infrared spectroscopy with JWST

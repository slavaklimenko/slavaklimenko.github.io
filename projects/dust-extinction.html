Research project 03

Dust composition and extinction

The composition and optical properties of cosmic dust from ultraviolet to mid-infrared wavelengths.

Research overview

I use JWST mid-infrared spectroscopy together with ultraviolet-to-near-infrared data to measure extinction curves, the 2175 Å carbonaceous feature, and silicate and ice absorption in galaxies at z ≈ 0.1–1.3.

Four JWST/MIRI programs (Cycles 1 and 3) target quasars whose sightlines pass 1–20 kpc from foreground galaxies, providing measurements of grain composition, structure, and size distribution in ordinary galaxies.

Observational approach

Combining observations across a broad wavelength range — GALEX, optical spectroscopy, near-infrared photometry, and JWST/MIRI — connects extinction-curve shapes with specific dust and molecular absorption features. Ground-based programs extend this work: the VLT/X-shooter SUENO survey and the Keck/NIRES PRISM survey.

Measurements

  • Ultraviolet-to-near-infrared extinction curves
  • The 2175 Å carbonaceous feature
  • Silicate absorption
  • Ice absorption
  • Mid-infrared spectroscopy with JWST
